About Me

My name is Jajuan Brown. I currently pursuing a BSc in Computing at UTECH. I am currently in my second year, academic year 2026/2027.

Short Autobiography

I am a 19 year old male, born August 17, 2007. I grew up and still currently live in Runaway Bay, St. Ann, but I stay in Kingston for the sake of attending school. I attended the Glen Preparatory School, NorthGate High School, and did 2 years of sixth form at York Castle High School, and at each level of education passed and graduated with exceptional honours.
I have many things that I do as a hobby. I love sports, especially cricket. I aspire to be a professional cricketer one day. I also love football (Barcelona forever), basketball, athletics, etc. Another one of my hobbies is gaming. I love playing games such as CODM, DLS, Geometry Dash, Star Defender 2, Super Smash 64, etc. I also love media production; video editing, photo editing, graphic design, I have done many personal and professional projects. I also love music, and currently play instruments and sing in a band called Core Expressions, made up of many of the young people at my church. I also write my own music and poems.
I also love to code. This was developed from a spur-of-the-moment decision during COVID where I did one online class with a teacher online about basic coding. It was only for an hour, but since then I haven't looked back. I began learning JavaScript, but stopped for other commitments. However, in 2023 I completed a HTML and CSS course for web development, and began learning languages such as C# (which I used to build a game for Animation and Game Design in Grade 13), C, and Python. Although really only prolific in HTML and CSS, I am still grateful to have learned the basics of the others. Currently, outside of my school course, I am doing an online certified JavaScript course, which I am nearing the completion of.
